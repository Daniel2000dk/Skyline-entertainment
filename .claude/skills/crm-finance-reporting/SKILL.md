# Crm Finance Reporting

Differentiate RP display names from verified identity. Keep registrations vs checked-in attendance separate. Track expected vs received amounts; append corrections instead of overwriting history. Compute money in integer GTA dollars and aggregate server-side. Test cancellation, refunds/adjustments, totals, permissions and zero-data dashboards.
